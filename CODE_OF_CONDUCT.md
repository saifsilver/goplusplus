# Code of conduct

We expect contributors and maintainers to communicate professionally, assume
good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and make participation
safe regardless of background or experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure
of private information, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.
Technical disagreement is welcome; attacks on people are not.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled confidentially to
the extent practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban serious or repeated
violations.
